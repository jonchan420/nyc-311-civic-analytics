# Unusual months

## What gets flagged
A month is flagged as unusual when a complaint type records more than 3 times its typical month. Typical means the median month over the 12 months before the period being looked at. Categories that usually have fewer than 20 requests a month aren't checked, since small numbers swing easily.

## Why it matters
A single unusual month can dominate a comparison. In Queens, Drug Activity complaints ran between 962 and 5,497 a month from January to October 2025, peaking in July, then fell to between 193 and 721 a month from November 2025 to September 2026. Comparing summer 2025 with summer 2026 shows a 93% drop that says more about those unusual 2025 months than about a real decline.

## What to do with a flagged month
Treat it as a question, not a finding. Possible explanations include a one-off event, a campaign or a few frequent reporters, or a change in how an agency records requests. The counts alone can't tell these apart.
