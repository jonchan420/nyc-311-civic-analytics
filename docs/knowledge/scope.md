# What this assistant can and can't answer

## Questions it answers
It compares complaint types between two periods, shows monthly trends, briefs on a single month, and reports the frozen forecasts and their accuracy. It covers citywide totals and the five boroughs, by month, from January 2020 to the latest complete month.

## Questions it declines
It doesn't answer questions about neighborhoods or ZIP codes, response or closing times, agency performance, individual complaints or addresses, or crime, which 311 data doesn't measure. It doesn't make new forecasts beyond the frozen ones, run SQL a person supplies, or claim to know why complaints changed.

## How periods are read
A season means whole months: spring is March to May, summer June to August, fall September to November, and winter December to February, named by the year its January falls in. A quarter means three calendar months, for example Q1 is January to March. When a question uses a period like this, the answer states the months it used.

## When it asks a question back
It asks when a place could mean several things (for example "the Heights"), when a complaint type isn't recognized, or when a comparison is missing a period.
